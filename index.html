Questions

Before you join

What is an Expert Advisor?
An Expert Advisor (EA) is a program that runs inside MetaTrader and executes a trading strategy automatically. It reads the chart, applies its rules and manages orders without you having to place them by hand.
Does the EA work with MetaTrader?
Yes. Guilherme EA was built for MetaTrader and can be installed on MT4 and MT5 accounts. Installation is done by adding the file to the platform's Experts folder — the full guide is inside the Telegram channel.
Can beginners use it?
Yes. The installation is guided step by step and the presets come pre-configured. We still recommend starting on a demo account until you understand how the algorithm behaves in different market conditions.
Which markets are supported?
The main focus is XAU/USD and major Forex pairs. There are also additional configurations for markets such as BTC/USD and US30.
Do I need to keep my computer running?
MetaTrader has to stay connected for the EA to operate. Most traders either leave the platform open on their machine or use a VPS, which keeps it running 24 hours a day without depending on your own connection.
Can I choose different risk configurations?
Yes. Each preset uses different lot sizing and risk parameters, so you can pick the one that matches your account size and how conservative you want to be — and change it whenever you want.
